Fairness Metrics Explained

Statistical Parity Diff
Difference in positive prediction rate across groups. Ideal: < 0.1
Equal Opportunity Diff
Difference in true positive rate across groups. Ideal: < 0.1
Disparate Impact Ratio
Ratio of positive rates (min/max). ≥ 0.8 = fair (80% rule)
